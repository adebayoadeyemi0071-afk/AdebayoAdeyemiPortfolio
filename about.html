My Story

"I started with a blank HTML file and a desire to make the web feel alive."

What began as curiosity turned into a career. I've spent five years obsessing over the details — the weight of a font, the curve of an easing function, the precise moment a page element should appear. That obsession is what clients get when they work with me.

I grew up in Nigeria, where creativity and resourcefulness are built into the culture. That background shapes how I approach problems: I look for the most elegant path, not the most obvious one. Every project I take on receives the same level of craft, whether it's a five-page marketing site or a complex design system for a funded startup.

Outside of screens, I'm drawn to architecture, film, and Nigerian contemporary art — all disciplines that have quietly influenced how I think about space, light, and pacing in my digital work.

What I stand for
01
Craft over convenience

I don't take the easy path. Every spacing decision, animation curve, and colour choice is intentional.

02
Performance is design

A beautiful site that loads slowly isn't beautiful. Speed and visual quality are equally non-negotiable.

03
Accessibility first

Great design works for everyone. I build with semantic HTML, ARIA, and keyboard navigation baked in from day one.

04
Honest communication

Clear timelines, direct feedback, no surprises. I work with clients as a partner, not just a vendor.

05
Continuous learning

The web evolves fast. I stay on the edge — currently exploring AI integration, WebGPU, and WASM.

06
Built in Africa

Proudly Nigerian. My work carries a cultural perspective that's distinct from the default Western tech aesthetic.
