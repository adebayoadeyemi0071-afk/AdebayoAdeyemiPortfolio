Common questions
What's your typical project timeline?

Depends on scope. A landing page typically takes 1–2 weeks. A full design system or web app can take 6–12 weeks. I'll give you a detailed estimate after our first call.

Do you work with clients outside Nigeria?

Absolutely. The majority of my clients are based internationally — UK, US, Canada, and across Africa. I'm comfortable working across time zones.

Do you only do frontend, or can you handle full-stack?

My sweet spot is frontend — React, Next.js, animation, and UI systems. I can also handle light backend work (Node, Supabase, APIs), or I'll bring in a trusted backend partner for heavier lifts.

How do you handle revisions?

Every project includes two rounds of revisions at each milestone. Anything beyond that is billed at my hourly rate. I try to align expectations early so revisions are minimal.
