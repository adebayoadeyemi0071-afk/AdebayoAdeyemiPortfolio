I build websites that feel alive — interfaces rooted in nature, precision, and the quiet confidence of good craft.

Based in Abuja, Nigeria, I'm a frontend designer and developer with over 5 years of experience turning ideas into polished, performant digital products. My work sits at the intersection of design systems, motion design, and clean engineering.

I specialise in building for Nigerian brands and startups that want a web presence as refined as their ambition — sites that move thoughtfully, load fast, and leave an impression.
